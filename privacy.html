How this website handles information

Contact and artwork inquiries

The inquiry form uses your own email application. Information you enter is not submitted to or stored by this website; it is sent only when you choose to send the resulting email. Email correspondence is used to answer your request and manage any subsequent conversation.

Local preferences

The site stores small preference values in your browser for the light or dark theme, whether the opening has already played during the session, and your Instagram-content choice. These values stay on your device and are not advertising cookies.

Optional Instagram feed

The Instagram feed is delivered through Elfsight and is not loaded unless you select “Enable Instagram.” Enabling it may allow Elfsight, Instagram or Meta to receive technical information such as your IP address and browser data under their own privacy terms. Select “Keep off” to browse without loading that service.

Hosting and technical logs

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with most hosting providers, GitHub may process limited connection and security logs when the site is requested. No analytics or advertising tracker is intentionally installed by Danny Hirsch Arts.

Your questions
